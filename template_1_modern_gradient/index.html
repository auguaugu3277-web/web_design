Paper Submission

Guidelines and process for submitting your research paper

Step 1: Prepare Manuscript

Use IEEE or Springer template
Papers should be 6-10 pages including references. Download templates from conference website.

Step 2: Plagiarism Check

Turnitin/iThenticate report required
Similarity index must be below 20%. Include the report with your submission.

Step 3: Submit Online

Via EasyChair platform
Create an account, fill details, upload PDF and plagiarism report.

Step 4: Review Process

Double-blind peer review
Each paper reviewed by 2-3 international experts. Results in 4-6 weeks.

Submission Guidelines

✓
Original, unpublished research not submitted elsewhere
✓
IEEE or Springer LNCS format required
✓
6-10 pages including all figures and references
✓
PDF format only, maximum 10 MB file size
✓
Plagiarism report must be attached (≤20% similarity)
✓
